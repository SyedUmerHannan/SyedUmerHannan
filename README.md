## 🚀 Featured Research Programs

### [Cardiomyocyte Maturation Engineering](https://github.com/SyedUmerHannan/cardiomyocyte_maturation)
*Developing mature, functional cardiomyocytes for regenerative cardiology*

Engineering clinically viable, functionally mature cardiomyocytes from iPSC-derived precursors. This research directly addresses cardiac regeneration—a critical need for post-MI patients with limited treatment options. Combines wet lab optimization with computational validation to create therapeutically relevant cardiac cells ready for translational development.

**Clinical Applications**: Post-MI regeneration | Heart failure treatment | Functional tissue repair

---

### [RegenAtlas](https://github.com/SyedUmerHannan/RegenAtlas)
*Cross-species insights into cardiac regeneration mechanisms*

Investigating conserved regulatory programs governing cardiomyocyte proliferation across regenerative and non-regenerative species. By understanding why some organisms regenerate while others scar, we identify translatable pathways to promote human cardiac regeneration—unlocking potential treatments for millions with heart disease.

**Clinical Translation**: Regenerative cardiology | Heart failure therapeutics | Mechanisms of cardiac repair

---

### [Chromosome 22 TDP-43 Knockout Analysis](https://github.com/SyedUmerHannan/Chromosome22_TDP43_DE_analysis)
*Molecular mechanisms underlying ALS/FTD pathology*

Decoding TDP-43 loss-of-function mechanisms to understand amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) and frontotemporal dementia (FTD)—devastating neurodegenerative diseases with no cure. Patient-derived cellular models and comprehensive molecular profiling identify therapeutic targets for drug development and clinical intervention.

**Clinical Focus**: ALS/FTD treatment development | Neurodegenerative disease mechanisms | Neuroprotection strategies
